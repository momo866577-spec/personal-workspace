# Third-party fonts

The webfonts in this directory are Chinese-only WOFF2 subsets. Latin letters and Arabic numerals were intentionally removed so the application continues to render them with its existing Geist/system font stack.

All five fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license text is available at <https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/> and from each upstream project.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `zcool-kuaile.woff2` | ZCOOL KuaiLe | <https://github.com/googlefonts/zcool-kuaile> | SIL OFL 1.1 |
| `lxgw-wenkai-gb.woff2` | LXGW WenKai GB | <https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenkaiGB> | SIL OFL 1.1 |

| `smiley-sans.woff2` | Smiley Sans | <https://github.com/atelier-anchor/smiley-sans> | SIL OFL 1.1 |

These files are modified subsets of the upstream fonts and remain licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. They must not be sold by themselves.
